\section{The Core Problem: Fluid Abstraction vs. Factual Verification}
In deep neural networks, individual units consistently exhibit mixed selectivity (\textit{polysemanticity})~\cite{rigotti2013importance, fusi2016why, elhage2022toy}. The mainstream interpretability community responds by training Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs)~\cite{bricken2023monosemanticity, templeton2024scaling} to unroll representations into dictionaries of isolated monosemantic directions.

However, enforcing monosemantic purity as an operational architectural goal is a conceptual dead end: it mistakes the core mechanism by which neural networks generate relational abstraction for statistical noise. Two fundamentally distinct cognitive functions are conflated:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Fluid Behavior (Polysemantic Units, $N_p$):} An internal unit co-activating across \texttt{IP}, \texttt{DNS}, \texttt{White Pages}, and \texttt{Phone Number} is not noisy: it has compressed diverse entities into an abstract relational invariant (\textit{identity-to-address resolution}). These lateral associative connections enable continuous analogical reasoning.
    \item \textbf{Verification Behavior (Monosemantic Units, $N_m$):} When returning an immutable literal — the IP \texttt{8.8.8.8}, CIDR prefix \texttt{24}, SSH port \texttt{22}, or root user \texttt{JOHN} — fluid interpolation induces hallucinations. The model requires a rigid memory cell with zero associative degrees of freedom ($\mathcal{R}(u)=0$).
\end{itemize}

Modern LLMs hallucinate because they entangle both regimes within homogeneous dense weight matrices~\cite{geva2021transformer}. HySA resolves this by decoupling fluid reasoning from factual verification, addressing the central engineering challenge of \textbf{latent indexing}: enabling fluid intelligence to query rigid literals with sub-millisecond determinism.

\begin{definition}[Relational Rank of Semantic Units]
Let $u$ be a neuronal unit and $\Omega$ the concept space. Let $\mathcal{C}(u) = \{c \in \Omega \mid \mathbb{E}_{X \in \mathcal{D}_c}[z_u(X)] > \theta\}$. The \textbf{Relational Rank} $\mathcal{R}(u)$ is the cardinality of pairwise associative transfer paths enabled by the unit:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{R}(u) = \binom{|\mathcal{C}(u)|}{2} = \frac{|\mathcal{C}(u)| (|\mathcal{C}(u)| - 1)}{2}
\end{equation}
A purely monosemantic unit $u_{\mathrm{mono}}$ with $|\mathcal{C}(u_{\mathrm{mono}})| = 1$ has $\mathcal{R}(u_{\mathrm{mono}}) = 0$. An architecture restricted entirely to monosemantic neurons cannot execute continuous analogical mapping without external relational graphs.
\end{definition}

\section{Unified Architecture: Neither Serial Nor Parallel}
Coupling $N_p$ and $N_m$ in \textbf{series} creates a mechanical bottleneck that prematurely collapses relational nuances; coupling them in \textbf{parallel} ($2\times \mathrm{FLOPs}$) requires intractable heuristic arbitration heads.

\textbf{Solution: Internal Dimensionality and Conceptual Cardinality $|\mathcal{C}(u)|$.} HySA operates on a single unified Hilbert space $\mathcal{S} \subset \mathbb{R}^D$, parameterized by conceptual cardinality $|\mathcal{C}(u)|$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{High Cardinality ($N_p$, $|\mathcal{C}(u)| \gg 1$):} Aggregate isomorphic concepts for analogical reasoning and fluid thought.
    \item \textbf{Unit Cardinality ($N_m$, $|\mathcal{C}(u)|=1$):} Metric centroids devoid of lateral branches, anchoring immutable facts.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Contrastive Superposition Tensors ($I_n$):}
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    I_n = \frac{1}{\sum_{X_k \in \mathcal{X}^+} z_{k,n} + \eta} \Bigg[ & \sum_{X_k \in \mathcal{X}^+} z_{k,n} \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{net}}(X_k \mid n) \\
    & - \gamma \sum_{X_j \in \mathcal{X}^-} z_{j,n} \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{net}}(X_j \mid n) \Bigg]
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:imprinted_tensors}, single units in a 4-neuron bottleneck ($N_p=4$) superimpose concepts in an orderly geometry: $I_1 \odot I_2$ isolates shared structural segments, while $\mathrm{ReLU}(I_1 - 0.7 I_2)$ isolates unique features, without exploding the network into millions of isolated features.

\textbf{Dual Temperatures ($T_p, T_m$) and Topological Temperature ($T_t$):}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.0pt]
    \item $T_p$: governs fluid Brownian exploration across relational manifolds.
    \item $T_m$: defines the radial rigidity and precision of Inode centroids.
\end{enumerate}
Their coupling defines the Topological Temperature:
\begin{equation}
    T_t(T_p, T_m) = T_m \left( 1 + \alpha \tanh\left( \frac{T_p}{T_m} \right) \right)
\end{equation}
bounding the closed metric ball $\mathcal{B}(\mu_k, T_t) = \{x \in \mathcal{S} \mid \|x - \mu_k\|_2 \le T_t\}$.

\begin{definition}[Metric Boundary Operator and Layer-$l$ Domain Gating]
{\small
Given intermediate state $q_l = h_t^{(l)} / \|h_t^{(l)}\|_2$ at layer $l$, index $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{mono}}$, and $d^* = \min_k \|q_l - \mu_k\|_2$ with $T_t < R_{\mathrm{dom}}$:
\begin{equation}
    Q(q_l) = 
    \begin{cases} 
      (\mathrm{CONFIRMED}, \mathbf{v}_{k^*}) & \text{if } d^* \le T_t \\ 
      (\mathrm{FAULT}, \bot_{\mathrm{Fault}}) & \text{if } T_t < d^* \le R_{\mathrm{dom}} \\
      (\mathrm{BYPASS}, \mathrm{Fluid}) & \text{if } d^* > R_{\mathrm{dom}}
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $d^* \le T_t$ triggers immediate \textbf{Early-Exit}, intermediate drift raises a \textbf{Semantic Fault}, and $d^* > R_{\mathrm{dom}}$ allows standard fluid decoding.
}
\end{definition}

If $N_m$ dominates with wide balls, space saturates and the model regresses into a static lookup table. The Critical Point $P_c$ balances fluid reasoning with surgical precision on certified facts.

\section{Empirical Validation on \texttt{Pythia-70M}}
Residual stream probing at Layer 3 ($D=512$) on \texttt{EleutherAI/pythia-70m} confirms three core behaviors:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Natural Polysemantic Isomorphism ($N_p$):} Probing four isomorphic prompts (\texttt{DNS}, \texttt{IP Routing}, \texttt{White Pages}, \texttt{Phone Directory}) yields cosine similarities between $\mathbf{0.893}$ and $\mathbf{0.973}$ (Table~\ref{tab:pythia}).
    \item \textbf{Deterministic Inode Injection with Early-Exit ($N_m$):} Indexing 6 technical constants with $T_t = 0.46$, HySA eliminates base-model hallucinations via an immediate mid-network Early-Exit ($100\%$ precision, $d^* \le 0.4304$, Table~\ref{tab:factual_benchmark}).
    \item \textbf{Immunity to Creative False Positives ($R_{\mathrm{dom}} = 0.65$):} On unindexed factual queries, $T_t < d^* = 0.4973 \le R_{\mathrm{dom}}$, triggering a \textit{Semantic Fault}. Conversely, open creative prompts land at $d^* = 1.0430 > R_{\mathrm{dom}}$, bypassing the index without false faults.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scalability and Intermediate Layer Domain Gating}
Downstream uncertainty gating on final logits fails symmetrically on open discourse and overconfident hallucinations. HySA resolves this fundamentally via \textbf{Intermediate Layer Geometric Gating}: by intercepting execution at layer $l=3$, creative flow ($d^* > R_{\mathrm{dom}}$) is preserved, hallucinations are intercepted prior to lexical projection, and certified literals trigger an instant Early-Exit. Leveraging GPU-accelerated ANN indices (HNSW / FAISS) in high dimensions ($D \ge 4096$), search latency scales to $O(\log M)$ in under $15\,\mu\text{s}$.

\section{Conclusion}
With \textbf{HySA}, I establish that polysemanticity is the foundational engine of fluid relational intelligence ($T_p$), while monosemanticity is the guardian of factual memory ($T_m$). By integrating both into a unified single-substrate architecture querying internal metric Inode APIs, models achieve creative abstraction with deterministic consistency.
