\section{Introduction: The Epistemological Dichotomy}
In deep neural networks, individual units consistently exhibit \textit{mixed selectivity}~\cite{rigotti2013importance, elhage2022toy}. The mainstream interpretability community attributes this to the \textit{Superposition Hypothesis}~\cite{elhage2022toy}: models compress more features than available dimensions ($M \gg D$), forcing non-orthogonal features to share representations. Consequently, significant effort has focused on training \textit{Sparse Autoencoders} (SAEs)~\cite{bricken2023monosemanticity, templeton2024scaling} to project latent activations into dictionaries of isolated monosemantic directions.

However, this pursuit of monosemantic purity overlooks a fundamental epistemological division between \textbf{Intelligence} and \textbf{Memory}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Polysemantic Units ($N_p$) Generate Intelligence:} Consider an internal unit that co-activates across the prompts \texttt{IP Address}, \texttt{DNS}, \texttt{White Pages}, and \texttt{Phone Number}. Standard interpretability diagnoses this unit as noisy or contaminated. In reality, this unit has compressed multiple disparate entities into a unified relational schema: \textit{the abstract concept of identity-to-address resolution}. Associative connections between otherwise distant domains are the precise structural foundation of analogy, generalization, and cognitive intelligence.
    \item \textbf{Monosemantic Units ($N_m$) Provide Memory:} In contrast, units that encode atomic, rigid factual literals---such as the IP address \texttt{8.8.8.8}, the CIDR integer \texttt{24}, or the root username \texttt{JOHN}---possess zero associative connections ($\mathcal{R}(u)=0$). Like a record in a database, an isolated monosemantic unit is intrinsically devoid of relational degrees of freedom; it cannot reason or generalize. Yet, it is \textbf{strictly rigorous}. This total absence of associative fuzziness makes monosemantic representations essential for truth preservation and factual verification.
\end{itemize}

Modern LLMs hallucinate because their architectures entangle both regimes within homogeneous, dense weight matrices~\cite{geva2021transformer}. During backpropagation, associative gradients continuously interpolate through rigid facts, causing the model to treat immutable historical and technical literals as plastic tokens subject to stochastic probabilistic drift.

\begin{definition}[Relational Rank of Semantic Units]
Let $u$ be a neuronal unit and $\Omega$ the concept space. Let $\mathcal{C}(u) = \{c \in \Omega \mid \mathbb{E}_{X \in \mathcal{D}_c}[z_u(X)] > \theta\}$ denote the set of associated concepts above activation threshold $\theta > 0$. The \textbf{Relational Rank} $\mathcal{R}(u)$ is the cardinality of pairwise associative transfer paths enabled by the unit:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{R}(u) = \binom{|\mathcal{C}(u)|}{2} = \frac{|\mathcal{C}(u)| (|\mathcal{C}(u)| - 1)}{2}
\end{equation}
A purely monosemantic unit $u_{\mathrm{mono}}$ with $|\mathcal{C}(u_{\mathrm{mono}})| = 1$ has null relational rank: $\mathcal{R}(u_{\mathrm{mono}}) = 0$. Consequently, an architecture restricted entirely to monosemantic neurons cannot execute continuous analogical mapping without introducing an external relational graph.
\end{definition}

\section{Contrastive Superposition Tensors \texorpdfstring{($I_n$)}{(In)}}
An isolated neuron in an intermediate layer produces only a scalar activation $z_n \in \mathbb{R}$. To understand the multi-concept polytope encoded by a polysemantic unit, its structural meaning must be extracted through the whole-network inference expectation over contrastive data.

Let $\mathcal{X}^+$ be a dataset containing target shared invariants (e.g., circular geometric loops or identity-resolution queries), and let $\mathcal{X}^-$ be a negative baseline dataset. Let $\Phi_{\mathrm{net}}(X \mid n)$ denote the whole-network inference expectation conditioned on active unit $n$. We formalize the \textbf{Contrastive Superposition Tensor} $I_n$ as:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    I_n = \frac{1}{\sum_{X_k \in \mathcal{X}^+} z_{k,n} + \eta} \Bigg[ & \sum_{X_k \in \mathcal{X}^+} z_{k,n} \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{net}}(X_k \mid n) \\
    & - \gamma \sum_{X_j \in \mathcal{X}^-} z_{j,n} \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{net}}(X_j \mid n) \Bigg]
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:imprinted_tensor}
\end{equation}
where $z_{k,n}$ is unit $n$'s activation for input $X_k$, $\gamma \in [0, 1]$ is a contrast suppression coefficient, and $\eta > 0$ is a numerical stabilizer. Peak-normalized rectification projects $I_n$ onto the positive semi-definite cone: $I_n \leftarrow \mathrm{ReLU}(I_n) / (\max(I_n) + \eta)$.

As demonstrated in Figure~\ref{fig:imprinted_tensors}, algebraic operations operate directly over these tensors:
\begin{align}
    I_{\mathrm{shared}} &= I_A \odot I_B \quad &&\text{(Shared Invariant)} \\
    I_{\mathrm{diff}} &= \mathrm{ReLU}(I_A - \gamma I_B) \quad &&\text{(Differential Feature)}
\end{align}

\section{Macro-Architecture: Neither Serial Nor Parallel}
How should $N_p$ and $N_m$ interact?
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Neither Serial ($N_p \to N_m$ or $N_m \to N_p$):} Placing a rigid monosemantic layer downstream collapses contextual nuance prematurely into discrete categories. Placing it upstream removes contextual cues before polysemantic abstraction can emerge.
    \item \textbf{Nor Parallel ($N_p \parallel N_m$):} Running duplicate independent networks doubles parameters and memory bandwidth ($2\times \mathrm{FLOPs}$), requiring heuristic arbitration heads to reconcile divergent outputs.
\end{itemize}
In \textbf{HySA}, the model operates on a \textbf{Single Substrate} in Hilbert space $\mathcal{S} \subset \mathbb{R}^D$. Fluid reasoning propagates natively through $N_p$ layers under temperature $T_p$. When generation reaches factual token positions, the system evaluates the internal Metric Boundary Operator $Q(h_t, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{mono}}, T_t)$ on-demand.

\section{The Semantic Hypersphere and Dual Temperatures \texorpdfstring{($T_p, T_m, T_t$)}{(Tp, Tm, Tt)}}
Rather than applying temperature as a post-hoc scalar divisor on final logits, HySA defines temperature geometrically as \textbf{the multidimensional metric neighborhood of a neuron in latent space}: the \textbf{Semantic Hypersphere}.

System dynamics are governed by two distinct temperature regimes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Polysemantic Temperature ($T_p$):} Controls continuous Brownian exploration and analogical drift across relational manifolds.
    \item \textbf{Monosemantic Temperature ($T_m$):} Defines the intrinsic radial rigidity and precision of the atomic centroid $\mu_k$.
\end{enumerate}
These regimes are unified into the \textbf{Topological Temperature} ($T_t$), which defines the closed metric ball $\mathcal{B}(\mu_k, T_t)$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{B}(\mu_k, T_t) = \left\{ x \in \mathcal{S} \;\middle|\; \| x - \mu_k \|_2 \le T_t(T_p, T_m) \right\}
\end{equation}
Formalized via a bounded saturating coupling ($\alpha \in (0, 1)$):
\begin{equation}
    T_t(T_p, T_m) = T_m \left( 1 + \alpha \tanh\left( \frac{T_p}{T_m} \right) \right)
\end{equation}
On unit sphere $S^{D-1}$, the Euclidean metric $\ell_2$ is in exact bijective correspondence with spherical angular divergence: $\|x - \mu_k\|_2 = \sqrt{2(1 - \cos\theta)}$.

\begin{definition}[Metric Boundary Operator $Q$]
Given latent state $h \in \mathcal{S}$, registry $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{mono}} = \{\mu_1, \dots, \mu_M\}$, and boundary $T_t$, let $k^* = \arg\min_k \|h - \mu_k\|_2$ and $d^* = \|h - \mu_{k^*}\|_2$. The operator $Q$ evaluates:
\begin{equation}
    Q(h, \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{mono}}, T_t) = 
    \begin{cases} 
      (\mathrm{CONFIRMED}, \mathbf{v}_{k^*}) & \text{if } d^* \le T_t \\ 
      (\mathrm{FAULT}, \bot) & \text{if } d^* > T_t 
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Geometrically, each Inode's decision region is a \textbf{Voronoi Cell Truncated by the Metric Ball}, $\mathcal{D}_k = \mathrm{Vor}(\mu_k) \cap \mathcal{B}(\mu_k, T_t)$, guaranteeing an unambiguous, disjoint partition of latent space almost everywhere.
\end{definition}

\section{Compression Gradient \texorpdfstring{($\Delta q$)}{(Delta q)}, Determinism \texorpdfstring{($\beta$)}{(beta)}, and Critical Point \texorpdfstring{($P_c$)}{(Pc)}}
How should capacity be balanced between polysemantic units ($N_p$) and monosemantic centroids ($N_m$)? We define the \textbf{Compression Gradient} ($\Delta q$) as:
\begin{equation}
    \Delta q = \frac{N_p}{N_m \cdot M}
\end{equation}
where $M$ is the cardinality of indexed literals. Given effective capacity $q = f(N_p, N_m)$, we define the \textbf{Degree of Determinism} ($\beta$) as:
\begin{equation}
    \beta = \frac{T_t}{q}
\end{equation}
Mapping $\beta$ against $\Delta q$ generates a convex thermodynamic potential landscape $\mathcal{E}(\Delta q)$ (Figure~\ref{fig:phase_transition}b):
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{E}(\Delta q) = \frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{drift}}}{\Delta q} + \lambda_{\mathrm{rigid}} (\Delta q - 1)^2 + \beta_0
\end{equation}
Since $\frac{\partial^2 \mathcal{E}}{\partial (\Delta q)^2} = \frac{2 \lambda_{\mathrm{drift}}}{(\Delta q)^3} + 2 \lambda_{\mathrm{rigid}} > 0$ $\forall \Delta q > 0$, the potential is strictly convex and admits a unique global stationary minimum defining the \textbf{Critical Point} ($P_c$):
\begin{equation}
    \left. \frac{\partial \mathcal{E}}{\partial \Delta q} \right|_{P_c} = 0 \implies P_c = \arg\min_{\Delta q} \mathcal{E}(\Delta q)
\end{equation}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Left of $P_c$ ($\Delta q \ll 1$, Under-Constrained Associative Drift):} $N_p$ dominates without sufficient monosemantic indexing. Associative trajectories freely drift across ungrounded manifolds, leading to rampant factual hallucinations.
    \item \textbf{Right of $P_c$ ($\Delta q \gg 1$, Over-Indexed Dictionary Saturation):} $N_m$ dominates. The volume occupied by constraints $\mathcal{V}_m \propto M \cdot (T_t)^D$ saturates the latent volume $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{tot}}$, freezing the model into a rigid lookup table.
    \item \textbf{At the Critical Point ($P_c$):} The system achieves optimal thermodynamic equilibrium. Polysemantic abstraction generalizes across isomorphic relations while monosemantic Inodes guarantee deterministic verification.
\end{enumerate}

In high dimensions ($D \gg 1$), the fraction of volume bounded by monosemantic constraints exhibits a sharp \textbf{Volume Phase Transition}: $\frac{\mathcal{V}_m}{\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{tot}}} = \frac{(T_t / R)^D}{1 + (T_t / R)^D}$.

\section{Experimental Validation}
We validate HySA across two complementary benchmarks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=1.5pt, topsep=1.5pt]
    \item \textbf{Synthetic $32 \times 32$ Manifold:} A 4-neuron bottleneck trained on dual-nature binary matrices (shared digit loops + rigid $2\times2$ literal checksums). As displayed in Figure~\ref{fig:imprinted_tensors} and Figure~\ref{fig:phase_transition}c, algebraic queries ($I_1 \odot I_2$ and $\mathrm{ReLU}(I_1 - 0.7 I_2)$) isolate structural invariants, while HySA maintains \textbf{$0.0\%$ factual literal corruption} across $T_p \in [0.05, 2.5]$ over 400 trials.
    \item \textbf{Real-World Transformer Validation (\texttt{Pythia-70M}):} Probing Layer 3 residual streams ($D=512$) on \texttt{EleutherAI/pythia-70m} confirms that isomorphic concepts (\texttt{DNS}, \texttt{IP Routing}, \texttt{White Pages}, \texttt{Phone Directory}) converge onto a shared polysemantic subspace with cosine similarities between $\mathbf{0.893}$ and $\mathbf{0.973}$ (Table~\ref{tab:pythia}). Indexing normalized Inodes in $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{mono}}$ on $S^{511}$ with radius $T_t = 0.46$ successfully confirms valid factual queries (\texttt{8.8.8.8}, \texttt{24}, \texttt{JOHN}, ports \texttt{22}/\texttt{80}, with $d^* \in [0.089, 0.430] \le T_t$) while intercepting unverified subterranean telemetry queries via a deterministic \textit{Semantic Fault} ($d^* = 0.497 > T_t$, halting hallucination).
\end{enumerate}
